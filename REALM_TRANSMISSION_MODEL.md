# Realm Transmission Model — v0.59.0

## Core law
No cross-region consequence exists merely because the source state changed. Effects require a plausible transmission mechanism and elapsed campaign time.

## Causal sequence
1. Source event occurs locally.
2. A transmission packet is dispatched through one or more networks.
3. The packet travels according to region-graph distance and network connection strength.
4. The destination receives information/material signals.
5. The receiving society may require additional reaction time.
6. Only then does the cross-region ripple affect prices, politics, security pressure, information pressure, or culture.

## Network timing defaults
- Information: ~2 days per region edge; no additional reaction lag.
- Security: ~2 days per edge; ~1 day reaction.
- Politics: ~3 days per edge; ~4 days deliberation/reaction.
- Trade: ~4 days per edge; ~3 days inventory/market reaction.
- Culture: ~12 days per edge; ~7 days social uptake.

Strong routes/dependencies shorten transmission time moderately; distance is never ignored.

## Stale information and correction
Packets already in transit are not recalled when reality changes. A crisis may be resolved at the source while distant regions still receive earlier bad news. Resolution generates a separate correction packet which must travel and be processed before stale ripple effects disappear.

## Design law
Reality, knowledge, reaction, and material consequence are separate timed states.
