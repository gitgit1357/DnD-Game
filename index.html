ROADWARDEN: SOLO REALM

A rules-driven solo fantasy campaign

DM Journal

Autosave checkpoints are written after every resolved fight, every Party Council session, quest completion/failure/abandonment, manual save, and when the page is hidden or closed. Character + Party saves now include the complete continuation state, including quests and quest history.
Visible rolls are shown. The campaign engine also resolves concealed checks for light, rumors, local history, dungeon patrols, imperfect mapping, faith, surrender, companion motives, luck, reputation, danger, suspicion, morale, and NPC intent.
