# v0.63.0 — Institutional Adaptation & Resilience Investment

## Core law
Regions do not only suffer hazards; institutions can learn, invest, maintain infrastructure, and change future vulnerability. Investments require campaign time and impose opportunity costs.

## Project lifecycle
1. Need is identified from recent hazards or socioeconomic weakness.
2. An institution or hero-backed coalition starts a project.
3. Construction consumes prosperity immediately and takes 45–75 days by default.
4. Completion permanently changes preparedness, infrastructure, recovery, food security, public order, enforcement, or bandit pressure.
5. The completed work becomes persistent regional history.

## Project families
- Floodworks & drainage
- Reservoirs & irrigation
- Crop wardens & pest controls
- Firebreaks & watch crews
- Winter stores & shelters
- Stormworks & reinforced roads
- Clinics & public-health stores
- Emergency grain reserve
- Road & bridge renewal
- Patrol posts & road watch

## Institutional behavior
Every ~90 campaign days, a region with adequate prosperity and no active project may review conditions and begin one recommended investment. Priority is driven by active/recent hazards first, then food insecurity, damaged infrastructure, and bandit pressure.

## Hero backing
The hero may back a recommended project with 30 gold. Hero backing shortens construction by about 25% and slightly improves final quality. It creates Chronicle significance when appropriate, but does not grant automatic fame.

## Guardrails
- One active/completed instance of a project type per region in this foundation pass.
- Projects cannot complete instantly.
- Investments cost prosperity while under construction.
- Historical truth and infrastructure persist even if social recognition later fades.
