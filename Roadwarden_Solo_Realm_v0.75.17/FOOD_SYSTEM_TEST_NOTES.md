# Food System Rebalance — Gameplay Test

## Design goal
Food should create expedition-planning decisions without becoming a repetitive tax.

## New baseline
- One Journey Supply feeds one party member for one day.
- Base market price: 4 silver.
- Weight: 1 lb.
- Town stock: 13–24 units before regional effects.
- Foraging: 1 hour, DC 9. Success yields one full party-day plus Foraging rank; ordinary failure may still yield a small amount.
- Hunger penalties begin gently and require several consecutive underfed days before becoming dangerous.

## What to observe
1. How often the player feels forced to forage.
2. Whether food purchases remain meaningful without draining quest income.
3. Whether large parties consume supplies too quickly.
4. Whether the easier forage makes Ranger/Wodewose investment feel pointless.
5. Whether hunger now matters too little.

## Likely follow-up knobs
Price, weight, market stock, forage DC/yield, warning threshold, and hunger-stage timing can all be adjusted independently after playtesting.
