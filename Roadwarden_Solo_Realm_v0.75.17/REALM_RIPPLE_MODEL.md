# Realm Ripple Model — v0.57.0

## Governing law
Regional consequences propagate through networks, not universal global modifiers. Distance weakens ordinary effects. Strong dependency, major routes, and inherently broad events can preserve more of a ripple across distance, but do not erase geography.

## Networks
- Trade: goods, shortages, prices, shipping, labor inputs.
- Politics: institutions, guilds, rulers, precedents, alliances.
- Security: raids, military supply, migration pressure, border instability.
- Information: rumors, news, recognition, public narratives.
- Culture: faith, custom, scholarly and ideological movements.

## Calculation concept
`ripple = source strength × reach × distance decay × network/dependency multiplier`

Graph distance is measured through neighboring regions. Each network has its own decay rate. Explicit dependencies can strengthen a path (for example Ironspine metal supply to Northwatch), but ordinary distant regions still receive less than the source and usually less than adjacent recipients.

## Current gameplay consumers
Trade-network ripples feed capped secondary market pressure. Political, security, and information pressure helpers are exposed for future quest, event, faction, and reputation systems.

## Design guardrails
- No instant realm-wide consequence from ordinary local facts.
- Distance attenuation is the default.
- Network dependency can partially override distance.
- Realm-wide effects require exceptional reach/significance or strong connected systems.
- Ripples are sourced from persistent World Facts and recomputed as those facts change.
