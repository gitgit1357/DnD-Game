# v0.61 Secondary World Fact Model

## Purpose
Delayed cross-region reactions can become new local realities, but only when accumulated pressure is strong enough to cross a network-specific threshold.

## Causal chain
source World Fact -> delayed transmission -> receiving-region reaction -> accumulated pressure threshold -> secondary local World Fact -> Director candidate / local quest / lifecycle

## Current thresholds
- Trade: 0.72
- Politics: 0.62
- Security: 0.68
- Information: 0.82

## Guardrails
1. Secondary facts are deduplicated by source fact + network + receiving region.
2. Secondary facts are marked derivedDepth=1.
3. A derived fact may transmit consequences, but reactions sourced from a derivedDepth>=1 fact cannot create tertiary facts.
4. Corrections do not automatically erase a secondary fact. Once local behavior has become its own problem, it has its own lifecycle and may require local resolution.
5. Secondary facts enter the normal World-Fact Director and may generate a local fact-derived contract.

## Current archetypes
- Trade -> supply hoarding / price dispute
- Politics -> external-policy backlash
- Security -> emergency patrol burden
- Information -> rumor panic / conflicting reports

## Design law
A ripple becomes a new local fact only when local reaction has become independently consequential. Numerical pressure alone is not automatically a quest.
