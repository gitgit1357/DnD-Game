# Realm Reaction Model — v0.60.0

Cross-region consequences obey four timed states:

1. **Reality** changes at the source.
2. **Transmission** carries a report/effect across the region graph.
3. **Arrival** establishes what the receiving region can plausibly know.
4. **Reaction/materialization** changes institutions, security posture, markets, or public information only after the network-specific lag.

## Consumers
- **Politics:** materialized political packets add temporary external pressure to receiving-region institutions. Corrections remove that pressure only after they arrive and materialize.
- **Security:** materialized security packets create regional readiness states and can increase travel difficulty by up to +3 DC while active.
- **Information:** arrived/materialized information packets create persistent regional news entries visible when the player reaches that region.
- **Trade:** remains connected to delayed market ripple factors from v0.59.

## Hard laws
- No receiving subsystem may read a distant source fact directly to create an immediate consequence.
- Source resolution does not instantly cancel distant reactions; correction packets must travel.
- Stale reports may remain actionable until corrected.
- Reaction strength is attenuated by distance and modified by network dependency.
